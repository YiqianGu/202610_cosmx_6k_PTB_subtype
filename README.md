# PTB CosMx 6K — subtype spatial viewer

Live: https://YiqianGu.github.io/202610_cosmx_6k_PTB_subtype/

Two specimens, 645,761 QC-passing cells, 6,175-gene RNA panel. Cell labels are
transferred from the 202610 combined PTB snRNA reference in **two rounds**: 10 major
types, then 60 subtypes restricted within each lineage.

| | PTB 24.3 | PTB 22.2 |
|---|---|---|
| cells (post-QC) | 436,574 | 209,187 |
| FOVs | 310 | 76 |
| IF channels | PanCK, **CD3**, Membrane, CD45, DAPI | PanCK, **CD68**, Membrane, CD45, DAPI |

## What to click

- **Cell type** — one hierarchical layer, opening on the 10 major types. Click the
  `▸` chevron on a row to expand that major into its subtypes and leave the others
  collapsed, so a **mix of levels** shows at once: TREM2⁺ macrophages at subtype
  level beside Fibroblast at major level. `Expand all` / `Collapse all` do every
  row. Hover any cell for its major, its leaf label and its round-2 subtype.
  This replaces the former flat `major` + `subtype` + six per-lineage layers.
  Note an expanded Myeloid is 13 rows and 9 collapsed majors sit beside it, so a
  mixed legend runs to ~22 rows — past the point where any palette separates
  categories, which is what the colour pickers are for.
- **Annotation Layer** also holds **Activation** (just the two populations of
  interest), transfer confidence, and sample.
- **Colour pickers** on every legend row; your choices persist in the browser.
- **`genes`** button on a legend row lists that label's **top 20 marker genes**
  (computed on this CosMx data, not the reference). Click a gene to colour cells by
  its expression. 300 of the 503 distinct markers are exported; the rest are shown
  struck through rather than offered as a dead click.
- **◎** rings a label's cells, or paste cell IDs into the highlight box.

## Caveats — read before quoting anything

- **The two slides are not comparable.** Different specimens, different panel build
  (`VnV`), different segmentation (`Initial Segmentation` vs `seg_2026_v1`), median
  cell area 31% smaller on PTB 22.2. A composition difference *between* them is not
  interpretable as biology.
- **CD3 IF exists only on PTB 24.3; CD68 only on PTB 22.2.** CD45 is on both.
- **`Proliferating`** is a single-subtype label that carries substantial T-cell signal
  (2nd of 10 on both CD3 protein and a T-cell transcript module, at 81% and 35% of the
  T-cell median). Treat it as a mixture, not a proliferation measure.
- **`PTB antigen-activated T`** comes from only 112 reference cells (1.5% of the
  reference's T cells) and is called here at ~0.19x the reference rate. Its abundance
  is a bound, not a point estimate, and it is sensitive to the anchor-weighting
  parameter. See `ANALYSIS_LOG.md` entry `[A01]` in the analysis repo.
- Subtype-level transfer confidence is lowest for the T lineage (median prediction
  score 0.415). Use the **Transfer confidence** layer before trusting a rare subtype.

Generated by `scripts/01_combined_annotation/08_viewer.R` (analysis node `[A01]`).

## The `- V2` row is not a cell type

`PTB antigen-activated T - V2` under T cell comes from **A02**, which re-applies an
archived rule: intersect the top 20% of cells on three gene modules (T lineage,
early activation, cytokine) and — on PTB 24.3 only — CD3 protein.

Measured on this data:

| | PTB 24.3 | PTB 22.2 |
|---|---|---|
| criteria | 4 (incl. CD3 protein) | **3** (no CD3 channel exists) |
| cells called | 1,460 (0.334%) | 1,165 (0.557%) |
| fold over chance | **2.09** | **0.70** |
| largest group by round 2 | Fibroblast 28% | Fibroblast 28% |
| precision vs the transferred label | ~1% | ~2% |

Three things follow. The rule's enrichment is carried **entirely by CD3 protein** —
drop it on PTB 24.3 and the fold falls to 0.92, i.e. chance. PTB 22.2 has no CD3
channel and never will, so its V2 call sits *below* chance and the two slides'
numbers are **not comparable** (3 criteria intersect to 5× more cells than 4 do, by
construction). And the set is mostly fibroblast, not T cell — its own author
concluded it is "not a usable per-cell classifier".

The round-2 `subtype` call is untouched and remains the annotation of record.
