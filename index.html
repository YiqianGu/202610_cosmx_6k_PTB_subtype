What to click. The Cell type layer opens on the 10 major types. Click the ▸ chevron on a row to expand that major into its subtypes and leave the others collapsed — so you can show TREM2⁺ macrophages at subtype level beside Fibroblast at major level, in one view. Expand all / Collapse all do every row at once. The dropdown also holds Cell type (T cell recovered) — on PTB 24.3 every CD3-protein-positive cell (65,570) moves to a new major, T cell recovered, expandable into the A06 screen candidates (antigen activation T_v2) and the rest; on PTB 22.2 (no CD3 channel) only the 543 candidates move, as one row under T cell — and Cell type (T cell recovered) v2, the same tree with A08’s candidates (antigen activation T (A08): activation list = TC9 vs other T ∩ TC9 vs TC10; 447 on PTB 24.3, 460 on PTB 22.2; only 400 of them are also A06 candidates; on PTB 24.3 it also carries E04’s exploratory antigen activation T row under T cell: 174 A01 T cells below the CD3 cutoff that still pass A08’s CD4 gate and module test — RNA-selected, so not equivalent to the CD3-gated A08 row) — plus Activation (just the two populations of interest), transfer confidence, and sample. Each legend row has a colour picker — your choices persist in the browser — and a genes button listing that label’s top 20 marker genes; click a gene to colour cells by its expression. The ◎ button rings those cells, or paste cell IDs into the highlight box.

Read these caveats before quoting anything. The two slides are different specimens on different panel builds and different segmentations (median cell area differs by 31%), so a difference in composition between them is not interpretable as biology. CD3 immunofluorescence exists only on PTB 24.3 and CD68 only on PTB 22.2; CD45 is on both. Proliferating is a single-subtype label that the prior run showed absorbs T cells, so treat it with suspicion rather than as a proliferation measure. PTB antigen-activated T comes from only 112 reference cells (1.5% of the reference’s T cells), which is why the reported percentage is a bound rather than a point estimate.

The - V2 row is not a cell type. It is A02’s re-application of an archived rule intersecting the top 20% of cells on three gene modules and — on PTB 24.3 only — CD3 protein. Measured on this data its largest group is fibroblast (~28%), not T cell, and it agrees with the transferred label at about 1% precision (8–9× over chance). Its own author concluded it is “not a usable per-cell classifier”. PTB 22.2 has no CD3 channel, so its V2 call uses three criteria instead of four and falls below chance — the two slides’ V2 numbers are not comparable. The round-2 subtype call is untouched and remains the annotation of record; hover any cell to see both.

The T cell recovered rows are SCREEN CALLS, not cell types. On PTB 24.3, T cell recovered holds every cell above A06’s frozen CD3-protein cutoff (the level only 1% of hypoxic SPP1+ macrophages exceed) — potential T cells whatever the transfer called them (only ~29% were A01 T cells; ~41% of the rest carry the Proliferating label, which hides T cells). Its antigen activation T_v2 leaf is A06’s Flex-calibrated screen (CD3 gate → CD4 gate → double-high activation + Th1 module test), which on the reference catches the activated granuloma effector family (TC9 + CD30⁺ effector + IL26⁺ Th17.1-like; family precision 0.89), not TC9 alone. PTB 22.2 has no CD3 channel, so only its 543 candidates move (RNA-gated, under T cell) — the two slides’ counts are not comparable. Hover any cell for its round-2 subtype; per-cell tiers are in A06’s candidate table.
